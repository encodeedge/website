### Curriculum Objectives
This lesson covers the core engineering concepts and implementations for:
- Foundational Milestones: LeNet-5 & AlexNet 2012 ImageNet Breakthrough
- VGG-16: Architectural Uniformity with 3x3 Filter Stacks
- ResNet Residual Skip Connections (F(x) + x) Solving Vanishing Degradation

### Overview
Trace the architectural evolution of deep convolutional networks across the ImageNet era, understanding how residual skip connections unlocked networks with hundreds of layers.

### Key Concepts & Takeaways
- Replacing large 7x7 filters with stacks of small 3x3 convolutions to save parameters and add non-linearities
- The degradation problem: very deep plain networks experiencing higher training error despite lack of overfitting
- Residual learning formulation: optimizing perturbation residual F(x) = H(x) - x around the identity mapping
