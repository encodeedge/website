## 🧠 Decoding the Terms: AI vs. ML

It’s common to hear **AI** and **ML** used interchangeably, but understanding the distinction is your first step toward mastery.

Think of it this way:

* **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is the **entire universe** 🌌 of intelligent tasks. It is the broad, encompassing goal of creating systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence. This includes everything from simple rules-based systems to complex neural networks.
* **Machine Learning (ML)** is a powerful **satellite** 🛰️ within that universe. It is a specific subset of AI where systems **learn from data** without being explicitly programmed. ML algorithms get better at prediction and classification the more examples you feed them.

At EncodeEdge, we believe mastery lies in understanding both the **theory** (AI) and the **practical execution** (ML).

<Callout kind="tip" title="Practical Takeaway">
AI is the overarching goal of intelligent automation; Machine Learning is the data-driven engine that learns representations from examples rather than static rules.
</Callout>
