### Curriculum Objectives
This lesson covers the core engineering concepts and implementations for:
- Classic Optimization Test Surfaces (Rosenbrock Banana, Beale, Saddle Points)

### Overview
Visualize how different optimization algorithms navigate challenging 2D/3D non-convex landscapes, observing first-hand how Adam and Momentum escape saddle points that trap vanilla gradient descent.

### Key Concepts & Takeaways
- Generating 3D surface meshes and 2D contour maps with Matplotlib
- Recording parameter paths step-by-step and animating progress
- Understanding the empirical advantages of adaptive learning rates on steep ravines
