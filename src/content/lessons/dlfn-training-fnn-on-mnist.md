### Curriculum Objectives
This lesson covers the core engineering concepts and implementations for:
- Loading and Normalizing MNIST / FashionMNIST Image Arrays
- Training Pipeline with Epoch Loss and Accuracy Tracking
- Validation Checkpointing & Multiclass Confusion Matrices

### Overview
Deploy your custom pure-Python neural network against the standard MNIST digit classification benchmark to observe training dynamics, loss reduction, and classification accuracy.

### Key Concepts & Takeaways
- Reshaping 28x28 grayscale images into flat 784-dimensional vectors and scaling pixels to [0, 1]
- Monitoring training vs validation accuracy to identify the onset of overfitting
- Computing class-by-class precision, recall, and error confusion matrices
