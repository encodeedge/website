### Overview
Transition from binary outputs to multi-class classification using the Softmax layer and understand why cross-entropy loss provides steep, non-saturating gradients during training.

### Key Concepts & Takeaways
- Numerically stable Softmax subtraction trick preventing float overflow
- Maximum likelihood estimation and information-theoretic cross-entropy
- Gradient simplification: dL / dz = y_hat - y
