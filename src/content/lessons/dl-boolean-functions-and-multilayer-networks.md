### Curriculum Objectives
This lesson covers the core engineering concepts and implementations for:
- Minsky & Papert's XOR Problem Proof
- Layered Composition of Linear Classifiers
- Implementing XOR and Arbitrary Boolean Functions with Hidden Neurons

### Overview
Understand the historical impasse that stalled neural network research for a decade and how the introduction of intermediate hidden layers unlocks representation of non-linear decision boundaries.

### Key Concepts & Takeaways
- Geometric proof that XOR is not linearly separable
- Constructing XOR using AND, OR, and NOT neuron primitives
- Layer-wise coordinate warping transforming non-separable inputs into linearly separable representations
